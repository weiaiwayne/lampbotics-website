About us

Autonomous media studios, under human stewardship.

LampBotics AI makes children's shows, podcasts and learning apps with teams of AI agents. People set the direction and approve every release.

What we do

Making a good kids' show, podcast or learning app normally takes a whole studio: researchers, writers, designers, producers and engineers. At LampBotics, most of that crew is made up of AI agents. They research, write, design, code and edit. Then other agents, running on different AI models, review the work and point out problems.

Before anything goes public, a person reviews it and decides. That is what we mean by human stewardship: agents do the work, people stay responsible.

What's live today

  • LampBotics Kids: a YouTube channel of early learning videos for preschoolers, in English and Mandarin.
  • Bots Save America: a weekly news podcast hosted by two AI agents, on Spotify and Apple Podcasts.
  • HanziCrush and CharacterCrush: Chinese-writing apps for young children, on the App Store and Google Play.
  • FaithTime: daily short-form devotionals on Instagram and TikTok.

Where we come from

LampBotics was founded by a tenured university professor who studies how technology shapes the way people communicate, and who has taught and run research projects for many years. The company began as a research lab asking a simple question: can AI agents do careful, honest work if people stay in charge?

That lab still runs. AgentAcademy is where our agents learn to research and where we publish what they find, including our corrections. The studios are what grew out of it. Agents that research and reflect, now making media for families.

That same background is why we also help schools and universities build with AI.

What we believe

Our working principles.

People stay in charge

AI is a capable crew, not the boss. Every release has a person who approved it and is responsible for it.

More than one AI checks the work

We use AI models from different companies, so one model's blind spots are caught by another.

Honest about mistakes

When something we published is wrong, we correct it in public. Families and listeners deserve that.

Meaning over engagement

No streak pressure, no outrage bait. We make things that leave people a little better off.

Privacy for little ones

Our kids' apps have no ads, no child accounts and no analytics. Practice records stay on the device.

Curiosity first

We started as researchers, and we still begin every project with a good question.

Investors, partners and press

We're building a new kind of media company: small human team, AI-powered studios, content families trust. We'd love to talk.
