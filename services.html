How We Can Help

Co-build projects with our agents. We bring the methodology, the multi-model infrastructure, and the hands-on experience.

What We Offer

LampBotics partners with clients to design and deploy AI agent systems for research and creative applications. We don't sell software licenses — we co-build solutions, working alongside your team to create something that fits your specific needs.

Autonomous Research Projects

Need to analyze social media trends, track market signals, or conduct large-scale content analysis? Our research agents can handle data collection, multi-model analysis, and report generation — with human oversight at every critical decision point.

  • Social media and platform research
  • Market intelligence and signal detection
  • Policy and regulatory document analysis
  • Academic literature reviews at scale

Creative AI Applications

Exploring AI for content creation, storytelling, or contemplative applications? We help design agent workflows that augment human creativity rather than replace it — tools that serve meaning, not just engagement.

  • AI-assisted content pipelines
  • Podcast and narrative generation
  • Contemplative and faith-based AI tools
  • Custom avatar and persona development

Agent Infrastructure Consulting

Already building with AI but want to improve reliability? We can help you implement multi-model validation, design guardrails, and establish audit workflows that make your agent systems more trustworthy.

  • Multi-model validation frameworks
  • CommDAAF methodology implementation
  • Agent guardrail design
  • Output auditing and quality assurance

How We Work

Every engagement starts with understanding your problem, not pitching a solution. We explore what you're trying to accomplish, assess whether agents are the right approach, and design a project scope that makes sense for both sides.

Our projects typically involve:

  • Discovery. Understanding your goals, constraints, and existing workflows.
  • Design. Architecting the agent system with clear human oversight points.
  • Build. Rapid prototyping with continuous feedback loops.
  • Validate. Multi-model testing and output auditing before deployment.
  • Transfer. Documentation and training so your team can maintain and extend the work.

Who This Is For

We work best with clients who:

  • Have a specific research question or creative challenge
  • Want to understand and oversee their AI systems, not just use them
  • Value rigor over speed (though we move fast when it makes sense)
  • Are comfortable with iterative development and honest assessment
