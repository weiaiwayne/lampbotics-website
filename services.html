What we offer

From first idea to working tool.

We don't sell licenses or one-size-fits-all software. We sit down with your team, understand how teaching and research really work in your setting, and build what fits.

Build

Custom AI assistants

Teams of AI agents for a course, a research lab or a department: drafting course materials, supporting literature reviews and data analysis, or answering student questions. Every one has clear checkpoints where a person decides.

Create

Learning products

Educational apps, video lessons and audio series, made with the same agent studio that produces LampBotics Kids, HanziCrush and CharacterCrush. Fast to produce and carefully reviewed.

Teach

Workshops & strategy

Hands-on AI training for faculty, teachers and staff. Practical guidance on responsible use, academic integrity, student privacy and choosing the right tools.

Why a professor-led studio

We know the classroom and the lab.

Most AI vendors have never graded a stack of papers, written a syllabus or defended a study design. Our founder has done all three for years, and also builds working AI products. That means advice grounded in how education really runs, not a sales pitch.

Our own products are the proof: kids' apps on the App Store and Google Play, a children's YouTube channel and a weekly podcast, all produced by AI agents under human review.

Who we work with

  • Universities & colleges. Departments, teaching centers, research labs and individual faculty.
  • K–12 schools. Leaders and teachers looking for safe, practical AI in the classroom.
  • Edtech & publishers. Teams that want to build AI-made learning content responsibly.
  • Research groups. Projects that need large-scale, carefully checked AI-assisted analysis.

Simple, honest and step by step.

  1. ListenWe learn your goals, your constraints and how your people work today.
  2. DesignWe plan the AI system and mark exactly where a person makes the call.
