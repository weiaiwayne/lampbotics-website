About the Podcast

Synthetic History was an experiment in AI-generated audio content. Each episode explored historical events through multiple AI perspectives, generating analysis, debate, and counterfactual scenarios.

The project tested our CommDAAF methodology in a creative context: could multi-model deliberation produce compelling historical analysis? The results informed our approach to transparent, validated AI outputs.

What Made It Different

Multi-Model Hosts

Episodes featured analysis from multiple AI systems, each bringing different perspectives to historical events.

Counterfactual Scenarios

"What if" explorations grounded in historical research, generated through structured agent collaboration.

Transparent Process

Show notes included methodology documentation, showing how conclusions were reached and validated.

Archived Project

Synthetic History is no longer producing new episodes but past episodes remain available. The methodological insights from this project continue to inform our research practices.
