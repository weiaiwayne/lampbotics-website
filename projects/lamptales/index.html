Legacy Project

LampTales

Narrative Experiments

Exploring the boundaries of AI-generated storytelling and narrative structures. Experiments in what happens when agents tell stories.

About This Project

LampTales was an early experiment in AI-generated narrative content. The project explored questions like: What kinds of stories do AI systems tell? How do multi-model collaborations affect narrative coherence? What happens when agents are given creative latitude?

While no longer in active development, the learnings from LampTales inform our current work, particularly in understanding how to balance agent autonomy with quality control.
