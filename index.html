How a LampBotics studio works

Agents do the work. People stay in charge.

Big studios need big crews. Ours are AI agents, so we can make high-quality shows and apps with a tiny human team, and a human still makes every final decision.

  1. ResearchAgents gather sources, check facts and study what learners and listeners need.
  2. CreateWriter, designer and producer agents draft scripts, art, audio and code.
  3. ReflectOther agents, running on different AI models, review the work and flag problems.
  4. Human sign-offA person approves, edits or rejects it. Nothing ships without that step.
  5. Publish & learnIt goes live, and feedback goes back into the next episode or update.
